The Ramen Bowl

A Culinary Meditation

The Foundation: Dashi

A great ramen begins with its soul: the broth. We start with dashi, the essence of umami. Drag the ingredients into the pot.

Kombu
Katsuobushi

The Heart: Men

Next, the noodles. The perfect ramen noodle has a firm, satisfying chew, achieved through careful kneading and cutting.

The Jewels: Gu

The toppings are the jewels of the bowl. Drag each one to create your perfect arrangement.

The Assembly

Now, we bring it all together to create the final masterpiece.

Itadakimasu!

Perfection. A meditation in a bowl. Before eating, it is customary to say, "Itadakimasu"—an expression of gratitude for the food and all who helped prepare it.
