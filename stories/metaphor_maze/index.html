Metaphor: "Argument is War"

We often talk about arguments using the language of war: "He attacked my points," "I defended my position," "She shot down my theory." Why is that?

Metaphor: "Time is Money"

We say "Don't waste my time," "I've invested a lot of time," or "That cost me an hour." This treats time like a valuable resource, much like money.

Metaphor: "Life is a Journey"

We talk about goals as destinations, making progress, facing obstacles, and finding our path in life. This frames life itself as a journey with a beginning, a middle, and an end.
