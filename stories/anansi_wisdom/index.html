Anansi's Great Idea

Anansi the spider, a famous trickster from West African and Caribbean folklore, was clever, oh so clever! One day, he decided he wanted all the wisdom in the world just for himself. "I'll gather it in a great pot!" he schemed.

A Tricky Climb

Anansi tied the heavy pot of wisdom to his belly and tried to climb the tallest tree. But with the pot in front, he couldn't grip the trunk! His little son, Ntikuma, watching below, said, "Father, why don't you tie the pot to your back?"

Wisdom Shared

Anansi tried Ntikuma's idea, and it worked! He easily climbed the tree. But at the very top, perhaps in his excitement or because wisdom is not meant to be hoarded, the pot slipped, crashed to the ground, and broke! All the wisdom scattered like shining sparks to every corner of the world, for everyone to share.
