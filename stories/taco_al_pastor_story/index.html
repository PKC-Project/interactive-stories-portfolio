The Spinning World

An Al Pastor Story

The Origin: Doner Kebab

Our story begins not in Mexico, but a world away. In the Ottoman Empire, artisans perfected a method: stacking seasoned lamb on a vertical spit to roast. The original doner kebab.

The Voyage to a New World

In the late 19th and early 20th centuries, waves of Lebanese immigrants brought this tradition across the Atlantic. They carried their culture and their vertical spit to a new home: Mexico.

The Fusion: A New Creation

In Mexico, a culinary alchemy occurred. The lamb was replaced with pork. Yogurt sauce and flatbread gave way to fiery salsa and fresh corn tortillas. The spit itself was given a new name: the trompo.

The Alchemy: Adobo

The greatest transformation was the flavor. Mild spices were replaced by the soul of Mexican cuisine: a deep adobo of dried chiles, achiote, and spices—smoky, sweet, and fragrant.

The Icon: Taco al Pastor

The result? Not Lebanese. Not purely Mexican. Something entirely new. A symbol of fusion, a story of migration, and one of the world's most perfect bites. The Taco al Pastor.
