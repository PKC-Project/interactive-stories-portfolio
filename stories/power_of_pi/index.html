Meet the Circle: Radius & Diameter

Radius (r): 30 | Diameter (d): 60

A circle is a perfectly round shape. The radius (r) is the distance from its center to any point on its edge. The diameter (d) is the distance across the circle, passing through the center. Notice that d = 2r!

Unrolling the Circumference: Discovering π

The circumference (C) is the distance around the circle. If we could "unroll" it, how long would it be compared to its diameter? Let's find out! This special ratio is called Pi (π).

Pi in Action: The Area of a Circle

Pi also helps us find the area (A) of a circle! Imagine cutting a circle into many tiny wedges and rearranging them. They form a shape like a parallelogram (almost a rectangle) with base πr and height r. So, Area = π × r × r = πr²!
