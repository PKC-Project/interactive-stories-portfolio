Mozart: The Child Prodigy

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, born in Salzburg in 1756, was a child of astonishing musical talent. Composing from the age of five and performing for European royalty, his early life was a whirlwind of tours, showcasing his prodigious abilities on the keyboard and violin. His early compositions already hinted at the genius to come.

Vienna: Operas & Symphonies

In Vienna, Mozart reached the zenith of his powers. He composed a breathtaking array of masterpieces, including his great operas like "The Marriage of Figaro," "Don Giovanni," and "The Magic Flute." His symphonies and concertos from this period redefined classical forms.

An Enduring Legacy

Mozart died young in 1791, leaving his monumental Requiem unfinished. Though his life was short, his prolific output of over 600 works across every genre cemented his status as one of history's most brilliant and influential composers. His music continues to be celebrated for its beauty, complexity, and profound emotional depth.
