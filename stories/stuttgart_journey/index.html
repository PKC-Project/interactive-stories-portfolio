Stuttgart

Zwischen Wald und Zukunft

Der Wald

Welcome to Stuttgart. Before you see the city, you must understand its cradle: the forest. The city lives within a green cauldron—der Kessel—forever tied to the nature that surrounds it.

Das Herz der Stadt

At the city's heart, centuries meet. Here, the stern history of the Old Castle stands across from the sleek modernism of the art museum. A local might greet you with a warm "Grüß Gott!"

Der Motor der Zukunft

This valley is the engine room of the automobile. The precision of Porsche and Mercedes-Benz was born from the local 'Tüftler' spirit—a culture of obsessive inventors who live by the motto: "Schaffe, schaffe, Häusle baue."

Die Kathedrale des Wissens

But the future here is not just metal. It is knowledge. The city library stands as a perfect cube—austere on the outside, but holding a vibrant, illuminated heart of books and ideas within.

Der Überblick

From the Fernsehturm, the world's first television tower, you see it all at once. Not a city versus a forest, but a city held by a forest. A future built on a foundation of nature. Auf Wiedersehen, Stuttgart.
