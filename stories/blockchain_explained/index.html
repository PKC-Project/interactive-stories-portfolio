The Digital Ledger: Transactions & Blocks

Imagine a digital notebook where every action or exchange is a transaction. Many transactions are bundled into a block, like a page in this notebook.

The Unbreakable Link: Hashes

Each block gets a unique digital fingerprint, a hash, from its content. Crucially, a new block includes the hash of the *previous* block. This creates a secure chain!

Shared & Secure: Decentralization

The blockchain isn't stored in one place but is copied across many computers (decentralization). If a hacker tries to change an old block on one copy, its hash breaks the chain. Other computers easily spot the fake! This makes it immutable.
