Random Access Memory is the chaotic, lightning-fast workspace. Data zips in and out, held temporarily for active tasks. It's the ephemeral thought process of the system.

This is the system's memory, the permanent archive. Data isn't just used here; it's etched into being, a slow and deliberate process of recording history for future recall.
