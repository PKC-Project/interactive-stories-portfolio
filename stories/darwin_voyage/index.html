The Beagle Sets Sail

In 1831, a young naturalist named Charles Darwin embarked on a five-year voyage aboard the HMS Beagle. This journey around the world, particularly his stops in South America and the Galapagos Islands, would provide him with a wealth of observations that challenged existing views on life.

Island Wonders: The Finches

In the Galapagos Islands, Darwin observed finches with strikingly different beak shapes. He realized these variations were adaptations to the specific food sources available on each island. Click a beak, then click the food it's best suited for!

A Revolutionary Idea

Darwin's observations led him to formulate his groundbreaking theory of evolution by natural selection. This idea, that species change over time due to inherited variations and differential survival, revolutionized biology and our understanding of life on Earth. His work, "On the Origin of Species," remains a cornerstone of science.
