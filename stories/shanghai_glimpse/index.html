The Bund (外滩 - Wàitān)

Shanghai's Historic Waterfront

Welcome to Shanghai (上海 - Shànghǎi)! Our journey begins at The Bund (外滩 - Wàitān), a mile-long stretch of waterfront showcasing grand colonial-era buildings. It's a testament to Shanghai's history as a global trading hub. Many Shanghainese people enjoy a 散步 (sànbù) here.

Pudong (浦东)

Skyscrapers and Modernity

Across the Huangpu River lies Pudong (浦东 - Pǔdōng), Shanghai's futuristic financial district. Its skyline is dominated by iconic skyscrapers like the Oriental Pearl Tower, Jin Mao Tower, and the Shanghai World Financial Center. It's truly 很漂亮 (hěn piàoliang)!

Yu Garden (豫园 - Yùyuán) & Flavors

Tradition and Taste

Escape the bustle at Yu Garden (豫园 - Yùyuán), a classical Ming Dynasty garden. Nearby, you can find delicious local snacks like 小笼包 (xiǎolóngbāo) and 糖葫芦 (tánghúlu). 很好吃 (hěn hǎochī)!
