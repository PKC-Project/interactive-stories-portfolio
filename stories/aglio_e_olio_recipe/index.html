Ingredients & Preparation

Ingredients:

  • Spaghetti (200g)
  • Garlic (4-6 cloves)
  • Olive Oil (1/4 cup)
  • Red Chili Flakes (1/2 tsp, or to taste)
  • Fresh Parsley (1/4 cup, chopped)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • Pasta Water (reserved)

Tip: Click the ingredient images above to "gather" them!

Let's Cook!

Step 1: Boil Pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add spaghetti and cook according to package directions until al dente (usually 8-10 minutes).

Plate & Enjoy!

Drain the pasta, reserving about a cup of pasta water. Add the spaghetti to the pan with the garlic and oil. Toss well, adding a splash of pasta water to create a light sauce. Stir in fresh parsley. Serve immediately, perhaps with extra chili flakes and a sprinkle of Parmesan (optional). Buon Appetito!
