PONG: The Dawn of an Era

Released by Atari in 1972, PONG is one of the earliest arcade video games and a true pioneer. Created by Allan Alcorn as a training exercise assigned by Nolan Bushnell, its simple two-dimensional table tennis simulation captivated audiences worldwide, igniting the video game revolution and paving the way for the industry we know today.
