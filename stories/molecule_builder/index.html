Meet the Atoms!

Everything around us is made of tiny building blocks called atoms! Different atoms like to connect, or bond, with each other to form molecules. Let's meet some common ones.

Building Water (H₂O)

Water is essential for life! A water molecule (H₂O) is made of one Oxygen atom bonded to two Hydrogen atoms. Let's try to build it!

Building Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) is a gas important for plants and our atmosphere. It has one Carbon atom bonded to two Oxygen atoms. Let's build it!
