A New Eye on the Cosmos

Galileo Galilei (1564-1642), an Italian astronomer, physicist, and engineer, dramatically improved the telescope. Turning this new instrument to the heavens, he made observations that would forever change our place in the universe. Try focusing his telescope!

Moons & Mountains

Galileo observed that the Moon was not a perfect sphere, but had mountains and craters. He also discovered four moons orbiting Jupiter, challenging the idea that all celestial bodies revolved around Earth. Drag the names to the correct moons (conceptual)!

A Changing Universe

Galileo's observations provided strong evidence for the Copernican heliocentric model (Sun-centered), challenging the long-held Ptolemaic geocentric model (Earth-centered). This marked a pivotal moment in the Scientific Revolution, shifting humanity's view of its place in the cosmos.
