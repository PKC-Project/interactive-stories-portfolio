The Apple & The Idea

Legend says Isaac Newton conceived his theory of gravitation after seeing an apple fall from a tree. He wondered: if gravity pulls an apple down, could the same force keep the Moon in orbit around the Earth?

Newton realized that the gravitational force between two objects depends on their masses (more mass, more force) and the distance between them (more distance, less force, by an inverse square law!). Try adjusting them!

Orbiting the Earth

Status: Ready

Newton imagined firing a cannonball from a tall mountain. With enough horizontal speed, it would fall towards Earth but continuously miss it, achieving an orbit! This is how moons and planets stay in their paths. Try different launch powers!
