A King is Born

From a stone egg on the magnificent Flower-Fruit Mountain, a monkey of immense power is born. Through his skill and cunning, he becomes their handsome Monkey King, Sun Wukong.

A Minor Post

His power becomes known to the Heavens. The Jade Emperor, seeking to control him, offers him a position. But the title—"Keeper of the Heavenly Horses"—is a grand insult.

An Act of Defiance

Enraged, Sun Wukong unleashes the Celestial Horses and smashes the stables, abandoning his post and declaring his own, much grander title: "Great Sage, Equal of Heaven."

The Forbidden Fruit

He is made guardian of the Peaches of Immortality. But when the Queen Mother fails to invite him to her grand banquet, he decides to sample the fruits of his labor... and gain their power for himself.

The Heavenly Duel

His crimes known, the Heavenly Army is sent to capture him. The great deity Nezha confronts the Monkey King, who is now surging with the power of stolen immortality.

A Legend is Forged

Not even the entire Heavenly Host can subdue the rebel king. His defiance shakes the foundations of Heaven, and his story will be told for ten thousand years.
