Space Invaders: The Onslaught

Unleashed by Taito in 1978, Space Invaders, designed by Tomohiro Nishikado, was a global phenomenon. Its iconic pixelated aliens, descending relentlessly, and the player's lone cannon created an addictive and tense experience. It revolutionized the industry, popularized the shoot 'em up genre, and even caused a temporary coin shortage in Japan!
